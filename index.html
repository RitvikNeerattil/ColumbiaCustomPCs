1080p (Full HD) is perfect for most users and gamers who want smooth performance with good image quality. It’s easy on your PC’s hardware, so you get higher frame rates without needing a top-end graphics card. 1440p (Quad HD) offers a sharper, more detailed picture—ideal if you want a noticeable upgrade in clarity and have a mid-range GPU that can handle it. 4K (Ultra HD) provides stunning, ultra-clear visuals with four times the pixels of 1080p, perfect for large monitors or professional tasks like video editing, but it demands a powerful graphics card to maintain smooth gameplay or performance.
